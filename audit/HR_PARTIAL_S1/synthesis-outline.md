# Synthesis outline

## Executive summary
Yes to half one, emphatically — at least a dozen implemented systems enforce a coherence axiom on AI
outputs, at both timings, across six axiom families. Yes to half two as well, but every published estimate
is unidentified: the reported signs disagree because the comparisons are confounded, not because the effect
is subtle. Only one system targets GARP, and generatively rather than by projection.

## 1. What Counts as an Intervention, and What Counts as Mere Measurement
Fixes the inclusion criteria before counting anything: an intervention *changes* the agent's outputs, a
measurement only scores them. Anchored on the operator/metric distinction that the rest of the report uses
to explain why reported signs disagree.

## 2. The Economics Front: Afriat, Varian, Houtman-Maks and What They Offer as Repair
Establishes that economics has had constructive repair operators for decades — efficiency, deletion,
money-pump and least-squares/minimum-cost indices, each with a constructive dual. Engages the tractability
tension: hardness is dimension-dependent, and deletion is polynomial at two goods.

## 3. The Economics Front Applied to AI Agents: Who Has Intervened
Shows the economics machinery has barely been pointed at AI agents, and that the one GARP-targeting system
works generatively — rejection-sampling a consistent training corpus — rather than projecting observed
choices. Includes the central-bank control-vector work as the closest economics-side neighbour.

## 4. The ML/Alignment Front: Coherence Theorems, Bradley-Terry Violations, and Nash Learning
Covered separately from §2, as the query requires. Establishes that coherence-implies-competence is settled
negatively in the philosophical literature and untested in identified form empirically — two distinct
failures that are routinely conflated.

## 5. The ML/Alignment Front Applied to Agent Choice: Enforce or Accept?
The enforce-versus-accept split, with the key correction: cycle-tolerant methods reject total orders on
existence grounds, not harm grounds. Commits on the cycles-as-defect-versus-structure tension, noting that
scalarisation demonstrably manufactures cycles.

## 6. Census of Interventions Found, Inference-Time and Training-Time
The report's structural centrepiece: a table of every intervening system by front, timing, axiom system,
whether the perturbation is formally minimal, downstream metric, whether that metric is exogenous, and
reported sign. The empty columns carry the argument.

## 7. Does Enforced Coherence Change Downstream Decision Quality? The Empirical Record
The contested section. States the positive results, states three failures across three axiom systems, then
resolves with target alignment between operator and metric — and concedes the adverse prior survives the
resolution. Corrects the length-uncorrected and mis-attributed figures circulating for the two ICML ablations.

## 8. Where the Two Literatures Miss Each Other
Convex special cases being rediscovered without the economics vocabulary, and the indexing asymmetry that
hides economics working papers from ML sweeps and conference philosophy from both.

## 9. Verdict: What Is Occupied and What Remains Open
The committed reading at full strength: repair is crowded, identification is absent, and the unoccupied
conjunction is named precisely. Ends with the residual search risk — the full-text venues that could not be
searched headlessly — stated as a limitation rather than omitted.
