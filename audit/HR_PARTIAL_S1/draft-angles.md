# Step 10 — three analytical angles

The corpus has genuine, load-bearing disagreements (six documented in `source-tensions.json`), so
the tension-based triad is used rather than the survey triad.

## Draft A — Strongest thesis: the field intervenes, and mostly successfully
Argue the position best supported by the weight of evidence: repair of LLM choice and judgment
consistency is an established, active practice — inference-time layers, isotonic projections,
aggregation rules, training-time penalties — and where it has been applied to the set its own
metric defines, it has improved or preserved downstream quality. The measurement question the
research query asks is therefore not "has anyone intervened" (they have, repeatedly) but "on what
object, and scored against what". Push the census hard and let the enumeration carry the argument.

## Draft B — Steelman the contrarian: repair is unwarranted, and the measure cannot bear it
Defend the minority position at full strength: enforcing a coherence constraint destroys
information the constrained object was carrying, and the index used to grade it is not reliable
enough for individual-level inference. Marshal the three published failures across three axiom
systems, the reliability evidence, the projection-reversal result, and the philosophical case that
coherence is neither rationally required nor informative about competence. Do not hedge — this
draft's job is to make the strongest possible case that the whole enterprise is misconceived.

## Draft C — Synthesis: boundary conditions, and what predicts which way it goes
Argue that both sides capture part of the truth and that the discriminating variable is
identifiable. Focus on when each side's argument holds: target alignment between the operator and
the metric it is graded on; whether the intransitivity is noise or an aggregation artefact; whether
the object is an agent's own choices or a third-party preference proxy; whether the model is
flagship or small; and whether the coherence treatment is isolated from representational capacity.
Deliver boundary conditions, not a split-the-difference verdict.
